InForce

Paste an AI answer, policy or document that relies on Nigerian tax law. InForce shows which claims rest on law repealed by the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, and what replaced them, on the date you pick.

InForce checks whether a provision is in force. It is not legal or tax advice.

Runs entirely in your browser; nothing you paste is sent anywhere. Statuses were computed by Cypher queries against the InForce law pack, a Relate graph store, and exported for this page. Built with Relate.
